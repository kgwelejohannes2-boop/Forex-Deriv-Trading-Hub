How signals work

The scanner uses 20 prior completed M15 candles for support/resistance, checks a breakout close, then a retest and a confirming final closed candle. SL is beyond the retest swing with a 0.25 ATR(14) buffer. TP1 and TP2 use 1.5R and 2R. WAIT is normal and not a trade recommendation. It refreshes all discovered synthetic markets approximately every 15 minutes while this page stays open; the initial full scan may take time due to API limits. Markets that fail to return candles show an error instead of a fabricated signal. No orders are placed. Test on a demo account and independently verify signals before risking money.
